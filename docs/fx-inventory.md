# Effects inventory

Every effect the web client draws programmatically, by kind, with its trigger, what it draws now, its numbers, its Unity status and its dev hook. Written to plan the effects redesign one effect at a time (the target is Marvel Snap's standard: the effect on the card, quick, overlapping, the sound on the frame the picture lands). Generated by a read of the code and a completeness pass; regenerate by hand when the effects change.

**Basis.** Read at `c7675d0` with the smoke and shut-door edits in the tree; every file:line was then re-checked at `9874de5` by a completeness pass, whose additions and corrections close the document. Since then only `bdac9cd` (Harriet's How to play panel) has landed, which touches no effect. Paths are relative to the repository root.

**Reader mistakes fixed along the way.** Spray gravity is `0.0011·t²` (Trails.tsx:494), not 0.0006 (that is the Stand burst, :284). `button.pulse` (styles.css:540–541) is live on the Sit Down button (MatchScreen.tsx:2481, 2513), not dead. The Ruling's "gavel" is the Threat tile's own ghost rising and striking (MatchScreen.tsx:959–998), not a card dropping from above as README.md:150 says. Fireworks fire only on a match win (MatchScreen.tsx:1590–1596; comment at :841), not on a Threat clear as README.md:146 says. The wave's leading stroke is the ring colour, not white (Trails.tsx:350 comment vs :358). `BoardFx.windup` is never set (`grep "windup:"` in MatchScreen.tsx: none). No `kind: 'reveal'` shot is built anywhere; no `reject=` prop is passed to `<Hand>`; no `.tpl-glare` rule exists in any stylesheet.

**Kind legend.** *follows-or-travels* = measures DOM rects and flies between tiles/Locations, or rides a moving card (stays in code). *fixed-on-tile / fixed-on-screen* = one spot (flipbook candidate). *css-motion* = transforms/filters/box-shadows, no particles.
