## Preset decks

- **Railroad** (`railroad`): Movement and organizing. Harriet moves people, Douglass and Organizer build a Location, Freeman and Stevens hold it, Ruggles names the informer, Smalls and Harriet together are The Pilots (+1 Relocation), Pleasant bankrolls the late turns, John Brown is elsewhere: Nzinga breaks the Threats. — Harriet Tubman, Frederick Douglass, Alonzo Herndon, Zora Neale Hurston, Organizer, Queen Nzinga, Sleeping Car Porters, Edmonia Lewis, Mansa Musa, Anansi, Reparations, Word of Mouth, Paul Laurence Dunbar, Bass Reeves, John Russwurm, Bud Billiken, Sojourner Truth, Robert Smalls, David Ruggles, Ayuba Suleiman Diallo, Katherine Johnson, Elizabeth Freeman, Thaddeus Stevens, Mary Ellen Pleasant
- **Black Star** (`blackstar`): Mobility and return. Garvey sends your Characters out Ready and Shadd Cary readies them where they land, Green and Smalls relocate freely, Nehanda comes back when pushed out, Peter Prioleau is planted on the other side to drain a Location, the artists leave Influence behind, and Asantewaa and Menelik close the late turns. — Marcus Garvey, Toussaint Louverture, Bessie Coleman, Nehanda Nyakasikana, Ida B. Wells, Victor Hugo Green, John Russwurm, Robert Smalls, Yemoja, Reparations, Peter Prioleau, Robert Duncanson, Community Defense, Mami Wata, OG, Madam C.J. Walker, Oshun, Empress Taytu Betul, David Drake, Organizer, Scott Joplin, Mary Ann Shadd Cary, Yaa Asantewaa, Menelik II
- **Uprising** (`caiman`): The cost flow. Washington, George Washington Carver, Queen Nzinga, Denmark Vesey, Nanny of the Maroons, Nehanda Nyakasikana, Marie Laveau, Bud Billiken, Ogun, Word of Mouth, Madam C.J.
